Most niche businesses do not need complex systems. They need a few reliable automations that remove operational friction.

## Core automation stack

- Lead capture to CRM with source tracking.
- Proposal and invoice flow with reminders.
- Client onboarding emails and checklist steps.
- Internal reporting dashboard for weekly decisions.

## Why this works

These workflows reduce context switching and improve handoff quality. Teams respond faster, close faster, and lose fewer leads.

Start small, monitor outcomes, then layer advanced logic.
