Scaling from zero to one in a month is difficult when teams build too much too early.

At Techbckp, we focus on a narrow first release: one core loop, one core promise, and one measurable outcome.

## What to keep in version one

- One user type with one urgent problem.
- One conversion path from first visit to first value.
- One backend architecture that can scale without a rewrite.

## What to avoid

- Features added to satisfy edge-case requests.
- Over-engineering for traffic you do not yet have.
- Delayed launch due to endless revisions.

The fastest teams do not build everything. They build what matters first, launch, and iterate from real behavior.
