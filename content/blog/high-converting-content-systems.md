A content funnel should not be a random publishing calendar.

Each asset must perform one of three jobs:

- Attract attention from the right audience.
- Build trust through specific proof.
- Convert qualified demand into booked conversations.

## Practical structure

Create a weekly cadence with one authority piece, two distribution assets, and one conversion-focused follow-up.

Track three metrics only: qualified traffic, lead quality, and booked calls.

When these metrics rise together, your content system is working.
